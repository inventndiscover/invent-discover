    Where PSHE comes from

    PSHE was created by Shishir Mehrotra while he led product for YouTube at Google, as a way to describe how product managers grow. Accounts date it between 2008 and 2011. He later co-founded Coda and is now CEO of Superhuman. Instead of measuring growth by how big your area is, PSHE measures it by how much ambiguity you can turn into clarity. In his words, "the best people create clarity out of ambiguity."
