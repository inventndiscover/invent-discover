Notice before you begin

What this is. An imagined view of the night sky as it might look standing on the surface of Mars: a dense star field, a thin rose-tinted horizon glow, and the two real moons of Mars, Phobos and Deimos, drifting past at their own speeds. It is a digital creation built to be plausible, not a photograph or a scientifically exact rendering.

Comfort. You stay in one spot the whole time — there is no walking or flying, so motion sickness risk is very low. There are no flashing lights.

In VR. Built for Meta Quest 2 and 3 in a browser. On a laptop or phone, drag to look around instead.
