Green Diwali

हरित दीपावली

Draw a rangoli, light diyas, wave a sparkler and set off a cone and a spinner, right on your own floor. No smoke, no burns, no frightened pets.

Notice before you begin. Everything here is virtual: nothing burns and nothing is real. It has bright, flashing light and loud bangs (choose Quiet or Pet-safe above, and you can switch any time). Use it at home with some space around you, standing still, not while walking. On iPhone, turn the silent switch off for sound.

🪔 Real crackers burn hands, frighten pets, babies and the elderly, and fill the air with smoke. This Diwali, light these instead.

On a computer?
Scan with your phone to light diyas on your own floor.

Rocket model based on "Fire Crackers" by Ankitimation; brass samai based on "Diya | Divli | Lamp" by ShonkV; both CC BY 4.0, reduced in size for phones. Cracker sounds from Mixkit and Pixabay (tanweraman, freesound_community), trimmed. The diyas, cone, spinner, sparkler, string crackers and rangoli are built in code, and the fizz, hiss and whirr are made live in the page. Music by Mikhail Smusev from Pixabay.
