Notice before you begin

1. Digital creation. This experience is a digital creation inspired by the Hanging Gardens (Pherozeshah Mehta Gardens) on Malabar Hill, Mumbai. It is not a real place and not an accurate map: the garden's layout, the plants, the wild honeycomb and the sounds are illustrative.

2. You are a honey bee. Everything is shown at a bee's scale: a marigold stands as tall as a house and the old rain tree is a skyscraper. The Tour flies one fixed, gentle loop from the hive, through the flowers and back, at a steady speed with wide, slow turns and a level horizon. Fly lets you steer. Switch between first person (the bee's own eyes) and third person (just behind the bee) at any time.

3. Motion sickness. Movement in virtual reality can cause dizziness, nausea or other discomfort, particularly during camera motion. Please use the experience seated or in a clear space, take regular breaks, and stop immediately if you feel unwell. Use of a headset is not recommended for children without adult supervision, or for anyone who is pregnant or has a condition affected by motion or visual stimulation.

Sound is produced by the experience and may be adjusted with the Sound control.
