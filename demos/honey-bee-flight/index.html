Notice before you begin

What this is. A bee's-eye view drifting slowly over a field of giant flowers, folded from paper in the same style as Origami Jungle: paper-grain petals, visible fold creases, and the white back of the paper showing through on the underside of a bending petal or leaf. It is a digital creation, not a real place.

Comfort. You do not steer. The flight follows one fixed, gentle, looping path at a constant slow speed — smooth curves, no sudden turns or climbs. On a laptop or phone, dragging only nudges which way you're looking, not where you go. There is a faint vignette at the edge of view while moving, which some people find eases motion sensitivity; it fades to nothing when you hold still.

In VR. Built for Meta Quest 2 and 3 in a browser. Your own head movement — turning, leaning, crouching — still works normally on top of the flight path.
