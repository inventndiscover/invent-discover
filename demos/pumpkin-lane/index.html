Full safety notes & controls
  • Flicker. Slow dips, under 3 a second, no full flashes. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or are sensitive to flicker, use Safe Light or skip this.
  • Motion. Use teleport or Motion comfort. Stop if you feel dizzy, sick or eye strain.
  • Space. Clear the area around you and set your boundary. You can play seated.
  • Props. Night, mist, masks, skull lamps, skeleton decorations, joke tombstones. No gore or chasing.
  • Children. Only as the headset maker's age guidance allows, with an adult nearby.
  • Health. Break every 20–30 min. If pregnant, or with epilepsy, a heart or other condition, check with a doctor first.
  • Way out. Safe Light (brighter, quieter, no flicker) and Exit at any time: A / hold B in the headset, top right on screen.
Headset: left stick walks · right stick turns · trigger rings or takes a sweet · Y settings · X performance
Screen: WASD or arrows walk · drag to look · click a bell, door or bowl
