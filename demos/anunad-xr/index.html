Notice before you begin

1. Digital creation. This is an artistic visual interpretation of tabla sound, not musical notation or instruction.

2. Flashing light. The light pulses and bursts with every stroke, up to twelve times a second at the faster layakari settings. Please do not use it if you are sensitive to flashing or flickering light or have photosensitive epilepsy. Ekgun keeps the pulses gentle.

3. Virtual reality. Movement and light in VR can cause dizziness, nausea or other discomfort. Please use it seated or in a clear space, take regular breaks, and stop immediately if you feel unwell. Use of a headset is not recommended for children without adult supervision, or for anyone who is pregnant or has a condition affected by motion or visual stimulation.

4. Sound. Real tabla recordings play when you press Vādan. Please set a comfortable volume first, especially on headphones.
