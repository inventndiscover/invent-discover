Spoon Race

चम्मच दौड़

The sports-day classic, in your own room. A lemon sits on a steel spoon and you race to the finish ribbon. The slightest tilt and it rolls; one jerky step and it's on the floor. Pick it up, put it back and keep going: every drop costs you time.

What's on your spoon?
  • Secure page (https)…
  • Real AR (race to a ribbon on your floor)…
  • Camera access…
  • Motion sensorasked when you start

Notice before you begin. You will walk while looking at your phone. Clear a straight path of 3–6 metres with no stairs, slippery rugs, pets or toys, and stop if anyone steps in your way. Everything is virtual: no lemons or eggs will be harmed. Best with sound on. Educational demo. Made for learning and research, not for commercial use.

🥄 Tip: hold your phone flat, parallel to the ground, like the spoon. When the phone is level, so is the spoon. Walk smoothly and keep your elbows soft.

On a computer?
Scan with your phone to race across your own room.
