Diya Carrier

दीया संभालो

Carry a lit diya on a brass thali across your own room to the mandir. Hold it level or the oil spills. Move gently or the flame goes out. When the wind comes, turn your back to it.

How hard?
  • Secure page (https)…
  • Real AR (walk to a mandir on your floor)…
  • Camera access…
  • Motion sensorasked when you start

Notice before you begin. You will walk slowly while looking at your phone. Clear a path of 3–6 metres with no stairs, rugs that slip, pets or toys underfoot, and stop if anyone is in the way. Everything here is virtual: no real flame and no hot oil. Best with sound on.

🪔 In real life, carry a lit diya on a plate with a rim, keep your dupatta and sleeves away from the flame, and never walk with one near curtains.
