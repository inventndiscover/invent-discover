Roop-Antar रूप-अंतर · Pradakshina & Avatar Museum

Walk once clockwise around a sanctum, keeping it on your right. When the circle closes at 360°, the gentle Saumya form turns into its fierce Vikral (Ugra) form.

A museum of light sculptures, not a temple. The figures are abstract forms made for learning, not consecrated mūrtis. In these traditions the gentle and the fierce form are the same deity: the fierce form protects and removes fear, it is not evil. Traditions differ by region and lineage, so each sanctum names its source. Please explore with respect.
  • Four sanctums: Devī ⟶ Kālī · Śiva ⟶ Bhairava · Viṣṇu ⟶ Narasiṃha · Sarasvatī ⟶ Nīla Sarasvatī
  • Forms brighten slowly and flames sway gently · Safe Light keeps everything steady
  • VR movement can cause motion sickness · stop if unwell
  • Procedural tabla: Teentaal in Ekgun near a calm form, Chaugun near a fierce one
Full safety notes & controls
  • Light. Each change brightens over about 2.5 s. Flames sway under 2 times a second. No strobes or full flashes. If you are sensitive to light or have photosensitive epilepsy, turn on Safe Light (steady, softer, slower).
  • Motion. Smooth walking with a comfort vignette and 30° snap turns. Stop if you feel dizzy, sick or eye strain.
  • Space. Clear the area around you and set your boundary. You can play seated and walk with the stick.
  • Sound. Drums get faster near a fierce form. Start with the volume low. M mutes.
  • Content. Fierce iconography is shown in an abstract way: dark colours, flames, many arms, a lion's mane. No gore.
  • Health. Break every 20–30 min. If pregnant, or with epilepsy, a heart or other condition, check with a doctor first.
  • Way out. Safe Light at any time (A in the headset, L or the button on screen). Exit with the Oculus/Meta button or Exit on screen.
  • Children. Only as the headset maker's age guidance allows, with an adult nearby.
