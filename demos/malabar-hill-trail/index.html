Notice before you begin

1. Digital creation. This experience is a digital creation, a representation inspired by the Malabar Hill nature trail in Mumbai. It is not a real place, and it is not a surveyed or accurate map. The route, landmarks, buildings and sounds are illustrative and must not be treated as real.

2. Motion sickness. Movement in virtual reality can cause dizziness, nausea or other discomfort, particularly during camera motion. Please use the experience seated or in a clear space, take regular breaks, and stop immediately if you feel unwell. Use of a headset is not recommended for children without adult supervision, or for anyone who is pregnant or has a condition affected by motion or visual stimulation.

Sound is produced by the experience and may be adjusted with the Sound control.
