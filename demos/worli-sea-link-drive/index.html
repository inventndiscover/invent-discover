About the Sea Link

The Bandra–Worli Sea Link (officially the Rajiv Gandhi Sea Link) crosses Mahim Bay in Mumbai, joining Bandra in the western suburbs to Worli. It is about 5.6 km long, with 4 lanes each way, and opened fully in March 2010.

Its tall cable-stayed section near Bandra hangs from towers about 128 m high; a smaller cable-stayed section sits near Worli. In all, 424 stay cables hold the deck.

A 16-lane toll plaza stands at the Bandra end. Two- and three-wheelers are not allowed. The speed limit is 80 km/h. At Worli it now joins the Coastal Road toward Marine Drive.

Built by Hindustan Construction Company for MSRDC.
