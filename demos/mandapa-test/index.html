Welcome. You are entering a hall of survivors.
These gods were carved and cast between the 6th and 16th centuries for temples and homes across India and its neighbours. Over the centuries many were broken in raids, defaced, buried, or carried far from the places they were made for. Some reached museums across the world; one was found in pieces in a village. Yet here they are.
Walk slowly. Each waits in the dark and lights up as you come near.

Safety notes and scan credits
No flashing light: each sculpture’s light fades on and off over about a second. Clear the space around you and rest every 20–30 minutes. With epilepsy, a heart or other condition, check with a doctor first. Children only as the headset maker's age guidance allows, with an adult nearby.
